🥗 Health · Tier-Based Framework

MAHA — Make America Healthy Again

A tier-based, evidence-anchored, zero-friction health framework for three groups who need it most: the overweight and overworked, the senior facing sarcopenia, and the average person drifting toward metabolic syndrome. Rather than prescribing a destination, MAHA prescribes a path — one cup of coffee, one peach, one minute of standing, sequenced over three years so the plan survives contact with a real life. Built on six independent scoring passes over the peer-reviewed literature and weighted 30/30/20/20 toward inflammation, digestion, convience, and circulation, the document ranks every food and habit by whether the evidence is strong and whether a real person can actually stick to it. Food tiers, synergy pairs, fasting protocols, grocery lists, cost analysis, drug-nutrient interactions, and honest caveats throughout. Not a supplement pitch. Not a fad.

🩺 App · Health & Biometrics

CardioSense

Most trackers force you to wait minutes for vague scores, streaming your private data to clouds you can't control. CardioSense is different. It's an elite computational laboratory that lives entirely on your device. By running proprietary cellular-mechanics models, CardioSense converts your phone's camera into a high-fidelity cardiovascular scanner. We move beyond basic heart rate tracking to deliver deep, frame-by-frame analysis of your vascular hemodynamics—quantifying pulse morphology and sub-harmonic variance to decode unmatched insights into your nervous system resilience. We don't just flag physiological stress; we diagnose its origin. Because all processing happens locally within your browser, your private data never touches the cloud. You get clinical-grade physiological clarity and actionable recovery intelligence for half the cost of other trackers. CardioSense is the honest, transparent telemetry tool for high-performance professionals. Stop guessing and start optimizing your biological edge.

🧬 Technical · Practitioner's Guide

GPU Programming for Biologists

A practitioner's guide to accelerating stochastic models, ODEs, and genomic pipelines — from NumPy to bare-metal CUDA. Written for computational biologists, biophysicists, and bioinformaticians who are fluent in Python but bottlenecked by CPU compute. The book covers CuPy, Numba CUDA, Triton, PyCUDA, and the cuBLAS/cuSPARSE stack; the warp-level primitives and shared-memory patterns that unlock real speedups; the trick of defeating thread divergence in stochastic state machines; and a full five-part production case study walking through a 2,000-line cardiac CICR simulator that runs 19,200 calcium release units in seven minutes and scales to a 32-cell electrotonic chain with GJA1 gap-junction modeling. Every pattern is grounded in biological analogy — ribosomes, polysomes, cytoplasmic compartments, extracellular space — because the biology is not a metaphor for the computer science; the computer science is a special case of the biology.

💡 Hardware · Off-Grid Engineering

TALA — Village Utility Device

A rugged, repairable utility device for the ~733 million people living without reliable electricity: two lights (a true 1 W walking beam and a ~160 lm family diffuser), an AM/FM radio with speaker, USB-C/USB-A phone charging, a 6,000 mAh LiFePO₄ pack fed by a 3.3 W solar panel, and a single-AA backup that takes over on a hardware latch when the main cell runs dry. The Rev A.1 package is a full engineering document — seven functional schematic blocks, cross-sections, an interactive WebGL model, a line-item BOM, cost and distribution economics, and an honest test-and-risk register that marks eleven items PENDING rather than pretending they passed. Stackable, packaging-free, IPX4, 1.5 m drop-rated, built to be fixed rather than replaced. Note: the embedded 3D model is CPU/GPU-heavy — it is best to close the tab shortly after viewing.

🪒 Hardware · Precision Engineering

VELRA — Precision Rotary Shaver

A full engineering package for a precision rotary shaver — sharp, softly. Three self-sharpening TiN-coated 440C rotors hone themselves against a 17-4 PH foil whose slot edges are deliberately asymmetric (sharp bottom to hone, R 0.15 mm top for skin safety), preloaded at 2.0 N by wave washers inside a ¼-turn twist-lock cartridge that swaps in four seconds without tools. Drive is a sensorless BLDC on Allegro's A89307 self-commutating driver — no firmware commutation table, just SPEED and EN — fed by a 14500 LiFePO₄ cell with onboard 1S protection, a BQ25185 LFP charger, and USB-C. The Rev C document includes third-angle mechanical views, a cross-section, four functional schematic blocks, a detail drawing of the honing interface, a 28-line BOM, and an interactive WebGL model with an Exploded and a Blades view that lifts the cartridge to expose the rotors and motor. Note: the embedded 3D model is CPU/GPU-heavy — it is best to close the tab shortly after viewing.
